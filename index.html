About Evigo

Evigo Technologies is a student-led startup focused on solving urban commuting challenges through smart, affordable, and sustainable transportation systems.

Our mission is to reduce travel stress, improve accessibility, and create intelligent mobility infrastructure for students and working professionals across India.

We aim to combine AI, EV ecosystems, ride-sharing, and smart route optimization into one scalable platform capable of transforming the future of mobility.

What We Are Building

Shared EV Mobility

Affordable shared transportation systems powered by sustainable electric mobility technology.

AI Ride Optimization

Intelligent route planning and ride matching for efficient and stress-free commuting.

Urban Smart Transport

Smart transportation infrastructure designed for future-ready Indian cities.

Scalable Platform

A future-ready mobility ecosystem capable of expanding into multiple transportation services.

Our Vision

To build India’s most accessible and intelligent mobility platform that empowers millions of commuters through affordable, sustainable, and technology-driven transportation solutions.
